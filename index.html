Privacy Policy

LiftRepLog — Workout Tracker

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LiftRepLog ("the app") handles information when you use the LiftRepLog workout tracker. LiftRepLog is designed to store your profile and workout information locally on your device.

1. Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, LiftRepLog may store the following information that you provide:

  • Name
  • Profile picture
  • Gender
  • Weight and height
  • Date of birth and age
  • Workout and exercise information you enter in the app, if applicable

2. How Your Information Is Stored and Used

The information listed above is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app's profile, workout tracking, and related features. According to the app's current design, LiftRepLog does not upload this information to a LiftRepLog server.

3. Sharing and Sale of Information

LiftRepLog does not sell your personal information. The app does not share the information stored locally in the app with the developer or other parties through an app-operated server.

Your device's operating system, backup settings, or other apps may handle device data independently of LiftRepLog. Their practices are governed by their own policies and settings.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains in the app's local storage until you delete it or remove the app's stored data. Clearing LiftRepLog's app storage/data or uninstalling the app will delete the locally stored app data. If your device or operating system separately backs up app data, copies may remain in that backup according to your device's backup settings.

5. Permissions

If you choose a profile picture, the app may request access to the relevant photo picker or media feature on your device. Any permission request should be used only for the feature described by the operating system and the app.

6. Children's Privacy

LiftRepLog is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information through online services. Because information is stored locally, parents or guardians should supervise children's use of the app and the personal information entered into it. If the app's intended age group changes, this section should be reviewed and updated.

7. Security

Storing information locally means it is kept on your device rather than sent to a LiftRepLog server. Please use your device's screen lock and security features to help protect information stored on it. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at the same policy URL, with its effective date revised as appropriate.
